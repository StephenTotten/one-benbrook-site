Two Places. One Shared Vision.

We have introduced ourselves. Now we want to share the vision that brings us together.

After introducing ourselves separately, we want Benbrook residents to know why we chose to run alongside one another and why we support each other's candidacies.

We share a vision of a City Council whose members come prepared, study the facts, ask thoughtful questions, communicate clearly, and remain accountable after the election is over.

We bring different backgrounds and experiences to two different places, but we are working toward the same goal: thoughtful, responsive leadership that puts Benbrook residents first.

What connects our campaigns is bigger than a single neighborhood or a single issue. It is a shared determination to raise the standard of service residents should expect from City Hall.
