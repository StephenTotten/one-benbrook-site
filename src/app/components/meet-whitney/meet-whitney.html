Meet Your Candidate — Place 6

Whitney Hughes

for Benbrook City Council, Place 6

Born in Benbrook in 1988, I've spent the majority of my life in and around this city. I attended Leonard Middle School and graduated from Western Hills High School in 2006 before commuting to the University of North Texas for a degree in Communication Studies.

My husband, Sam, and I have one son, and we're thrilled to continue calling Benbrook home. I'm a proud BASIS Benbrook mom!

I enjoy playing the piano, embroidery, photography, attending renaissance faires, and swimming.
